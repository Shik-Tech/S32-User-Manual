Snapshots ​

A snapshot is a scene you can call back in one press: the intro, the drop, the breakdown. Each bank has 32 pads.

BankStoresOpen it from
KnobsAll 32 knob positionsMain Menu → SNAPSHOTS
A rackThat rack's device settingsRacks list → hold Hold Select (long press) Select on the rack → SNAPSHOTS BANK
S32 LFOsAll LFO settingsS32 LFOs grid → hold Hold Select (long press) Select → SNAPSHOTS BANK

Every bank works the same way: Knob snapshots covers take, recall and the pad menu.

  • Morph glides or blends from what you hear to a pad.
  • Arrange plays snapshots from every bank as a song.

Snapshots and Arrange are part of the project. Takes, names and edits last until power-off; SAVE PROJECT keeps them (Projects).

Keep something out of snapshots ​

Some things you want to play by hand, like a filter cutoff you ride or an LFO rate you nudge. Set them to IN SNAPSHOT OFF and no recall, fade or morph moves them. TAKE still stores them, so switching back to ON loses nothing.

One parameter ​

  1. In a device or LFO parameter list, hold Hold Select (long press) Select on the parameter.
  2. Press Press Select (short) Select on IN SNAPSHOT to switch it to OFF.

ALLOW MORPH sits under IN SNAPSHOT while that is ON. Press Press Select (short) Select on it to switch between YES and NO. It decides how the parameter moves during a fade or morph:

SettingDuring a fade or morph
YESA value with a range glides. An on/off or choice switches at 50%
NOStays put, then jumps to the scene at 100%

Values with a range start at YES. On/off and choice rows (BYPASS, SYNC, MODE, SCALE…) start at NO, so they don't flip halfway through a blend; an LFO's WAVETABLE starts at YES.

A whole macro, device or LFO ​

ForHold Select onThen
A macroThe macro row in edit modeIN SNAPSHOT
A deviceThe device card in the rackIN SNAPSHOT
An S32 LFOThe LFO on the S32 LFOs gridIN SNAPSHOT
A CV16 LFOThe LFO in CV16 Module → CV16 LFOsIN SNAPSHOT

While a whole device or LFO is out, its parameters have no IN SNAPSHOT row of their own. A device that is out is marked on its rack card.

SNAPSHOT and MORPH macros are never part of a snapshot, so they have no IN SNAPSHOT row.
