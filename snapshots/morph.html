Morph ​

A morph takes you from what you hear now to a snapshot pad, smoothly. It works on every bank: knobs, racks and LFOs.

Glide to a pad ​

Set the bank's FADE TIME on its pencil tab, then press Press Select (short) Select on a filled pad. The scene glides in over that time, at the project tempo. With FADE TIME OFF it lands at once.

Pads, MIDI and snapshot knobs glide this way. Arrange uses its own FADE TIME.

Press another filled pad during a glide and it heads there instead, from what you hear at that moment.

Blend by hand ​

Hold Hold Select (long press) Select on a filled pad and turn: 0% is your sound, 100% is the pad. Turn faster for bigger steps.

  • Let go below 100% and the blend stays there. Hold Select and turn again to carry on.
  • Let go at 100% and the pad becomes the current scene.
  • Turn back to 0% and the morph ends. You hear your sound again, with anything you changed on the way.

This works with FADE TIME OFF too, and during a glide.

Moving a knob or a setting during a morph keeps your value. The morph stops moving it.

Map the amount to a knob ​

On the bank's pencil tab, choose MAPPING → MAP MORPH. Pick a knob (INTERNAL MAPPING) or learn a CC or 14-bit CC (EXTERNAL MAPPING).

The mapped knob is a crossfader: 0% is your sound, 100% is the bank's current snapshot. Recall another pad and the knob aims at that one.

On the macro grid, PART picks the bank (KNOBS, LFOS, RACK1–RACK8). INVERT makes turning down morph up.

The knob never jumps the sound. When it doesn't match the amount, turn it to the mark on the bar to pick it up.

MAPPING → UNMAP MORPH removes the knob. To drop a learned CC, choose MAP MORPH → EXTERNAL MAPPING again.

What a morph moves ​

  • Knob positions, LFO rate, depth, phase and offset, and device values with a range glide all the way, unless their ALLOW MORPH is NO.
  • On/off values and choices switch in one step, at the point their ALLOW MORPH sets.
  • Notes and channels switch at 100%.
  • A Delay DELAY TIME, an Arpeggiator RATE or an Envelope time with SYNC on moves through note values only. When SYNC differs between your sound and the pad, the time jumps to the pad's length at 50%; SYNC follows at its own ALLOW MORPH point.
  • A Step Seq pattern switches as a whole at 50%; the playhead keeps running.
  • Each LFO keeps moving its targets during the morph.
  • Anything set to IN SNAPSHOT OFF stays put.

While a bank glides, its pads can't be taken, deleted, copied, swapped or moved. The screen says WAIT FOR THE GLIDE TO END; try again when it ends.
