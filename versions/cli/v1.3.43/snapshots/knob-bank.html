Knob snapshots ​

A knob snapshot stores the position of all 32 knobs. Take one for each part of your set, then jump between them while you play.

Open Main Menu → SNAPSHOTS. The grid holds 32 pads on two pages of 16.

Everything on this page works the same on rack snapshots and LFO snapshots.

Take a snapshot ​

  1. Set the knobs the way you want the scene.
  2. Go to an empty pad and hold Hold Select (long press) Select.
  3. Choose TAKE A SNAPSHOT.

It's named after the pad (S7 on pad 07); rename it from the pad menu.

Recall a snapshot ​

Press Press Select (short) Select on a filled pad. The scene glides in over this bank's FADE TIME, or lands at once when it is OFF.

  • A * after the name means you've changed this bank since the last take or recall.
  • A pulsing pad is waiting for SYNC or an Arrange QUEUE. Leaving the grid doesn't cancel it. Press a pad that waits for SYNC again to cancel it; cancel a QUEUE on the Arrange pencil tab.

Pad menu ​

Hold Hold Select (long press) Select on a filled pad:

  • RETAKE A SNAPSHOT stores the knobs as they are now on this pad.
  • EDIT opens RENAME, COPY, SWAP, MOVE and DELETE.

Turn while holding Select to blend toward the pad by hand instead: see Morph.

For COPY, SWAP and MOVE, go to the destination pad and press Select. A filled destination asks OVERWRITE THIS SNAPSHOT? To cancel, press Select on the source pad again, or hold Select + Back.

  • COPY keeps the source.
  • MOVE empties the source.
  • SWAP trades two pads.

Pencil tab ​

Hold Hold Back and Select together Select + Back to switch between the pads and the pencil tab.

RowWhat it does
SYNCWhen a recalled scene lands: IMMEDIATE (default), or on the next 1 BAR, 2 BARS, 3 BARS or 4 BARS. With the clock stopped, the scene lands at once
FADE TIMEHow long the scene glides in: OFF (default), 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1 BAR, 2 BARS, 4 BARS
MAPPINGControl this bank from a knob or from MIDI

To change a value: Edit a value.

Choosing another pad while one is waiting keeps the same bar; the count doesn't restart.

Map a knob or MIDI to scenes ​

MAPPING → MAP SNAPSHOT:

  • RECALL SLOT → INTERNAL MAPPING turns a knob into a scene picker.
  • RECALL SLOT → EXTERNAL MAPPING learns MIDI. Program Change 0–31 and notes 0–31 pick pads 01–32. A CC moves through the pads. Other notes still reach your racks.
  • NEXT SNAPSHOT and PREV SNAPSHOT learn a button that steps to the next or previous filled pad, and goes round at the end.

On a snapshot knob, MIN and MAX (1–32) limit the pads it reaches, and it skips empty pads: with scenes on 2, 3 and 5, a full turn goes 2, 3, 5.

MAPPING → MAP MORPH maps the blend amount instead. See Morph.

To leave one knob's macro alone on every recall, see Keep something out of snapshots.
