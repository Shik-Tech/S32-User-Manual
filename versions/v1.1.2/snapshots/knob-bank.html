Knob snapshots ​

A knob snapshot stores the position of all 32 knobs. Take one for each part of your set, then jump between them while you play.

Open Main Menu → SNAPSHOTS → KNOBS. The grid holds 32 pads on two pages of 16.

Everything on this page works the same on rack snapshots and LFO snapshots.

Take a snapshot ​

  1. Set the knobs the way you want the scene.
  2. Go to an empty pad and hold Hold Select (long press) Select.
  3. Choose TAKE A SNAPSHOT.

It's named after the pad (S7 on pad 07); rename it from the pad menu.

Recall a snapshot ​

Press Press Select (short) Select on a filled pad. The scene glides in over this bank's FADE TIME, or lands at once when it is OFF. This works on the current pad too: it glides back over FADE TIME.

Recalling a knob snapshot sets every parameter a knob controls to the knob's stored position, even when the knob does not move. A knob's SYNC is set before its RATE.

  • A * after the name means you've changed this bank since the last take or recall.
  • A pulsing pad is waiting for SYNC or an Arrange QUEUE. Leaving the grid doesn't cancel it. Press a pad that waits for SYNC again to cancel it; cancel a QUEUE on the Arrange pencil tab.

Pad menu ​

Hold Hold Select (long press) Select on a filled pad:

  • RETAKE A SNAPSHOT stores the knobs as they are now on this pad.
  • EDIT opens RENAME, COPY, SWAP, MOVE and DELETE.
  • MAP PAD learns one MIDI message for this pad alone; see Map a knob or MIDI to scenes.

Turn while holding Select to blend toward the pad by hand instead: see Morph.

For COPY, SWAP and MOVE, go to the destination pad and press Select. A filled destination asks OVERWRITE THIS SNAPSHOT? To cancel, press Select on the source pad again, or hold Select + Back. Leaving the bank cancels it too.

  • COPY keeps the source.
  • MOVE empties the source.
  • SWAP trades two pads.

Pencil tab ​

Hold Hold Back and Select together Select + Back to switch between the pads and the pencil tab.

RowWhat it does
SYNCWhen a recalled scene lands: IMMEDIATE (default), or on the next 1 BAR, 2 BARS, 3 BARS or 4 BARS. With the clock stopped, the scene lands at once
FADE TIMEHow long the scene glides in: OFF (default), 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1 BAR, 2 BARS, 4 BARS
MAPPINGControl this bank from a knob or from MIDI

To change a value: Edit a value.

Choosing another pad while one is waiting keeps the same bar; the count doesn't restart.

Map a knob or MIDI to scenes ​

MAPPING → MAP SNAPSHOT:

  • A mapped row shows its source on the right: the arrow and CC number (or note name) for MIDI, the knob mark for a knob. An unmapped row shows nothing.
  • RECALL SLOT → INTERNAL MAPPING turns a knob into a scene picker.
  • RECALL SLOT → EXTERNAL MAPPING learns MIDI. One note or Program Change maps the whole bank in order: the prompt says SEND THE NOTE OR PROGRAM CHANGE FOR PAD 01, OR A CC TO MOVE THROUGH THE PADS. The number you send is pad 01, and the next 31 numbers are the pads after it: note E1 fills pads 01–32, and PC 110 fills pads 01–18. A CC moves through the pads. Notes above the bank still reach your racks.
  • MAP PAD on a pad's Options learns one message for that pad alone. Hold Select on the pad and choose MAP PAD. That pad then ignores the bank's number and the CC sweep, so a pad can keep its own button or note.
  • Learning a whole bank over pads that have their own mapping asks REPLACES N PAD MAPPINGS first. REPLACE removes them when the new message is learned; BACK keeps everything as it was.
  • NEXT SNAPSHOT and PREV SNAPSHOT learn a button that steps to the next or previous filled pad, and goes round at the end.
  • Select a mapped row to open LEARN AGAIN / REMOVE MAPPING, titled with its source (for example CH3 CC34, or CH2 E1+ for a whole bank). Nothing is removed until you choose REMOVE MAPPING; LEARN AGAIN keeps the old mapping if you press Back without sending a message.
  • EXTERNAL MAPPING on a target that a knob already controls says ALREADY MAPPED TO KNOB 05, MACRO 3 and changes nothing. INTERNAL MAPPING → REMOVE MAPPING removes only the knob mapping, never a MIDI mapping.
  • A full mapping list says MAPPINGS FULL and does not start learning.
  • In one bank, the last mapping you learn takes the key and replaces the old one; the same key can still drive other banks. The panel says what the key does now and what lost it, for example F3 NOW TRIGGERS THE NEXT SNAPSHOT. PAD 06 NO LONGER HAS A KEY.
  • When one key recalls pads in more than one bank, the banks change one after the other, and the knob bank changes last.

On a snapshot knob, MIN and MAX (1–32) limit the pads it reaches, and it skips empty pads: with scenes on 2, 3 and 5, a full turn goes 2, 3, 5.

MAPPING → MAP MORPH maps the blend amount instead. It shows its source on the bank's MAPPING list, and a mapped EXTERNAL MAPPING opens the same LEARN AGAIN / REMOVE MAPPING menu. See Morph.

To leave one knob's macro alone on every recall, see Keep something out of snapshots.
