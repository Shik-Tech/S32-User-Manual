Rack snapshots ​

A rack snapshot stores the settings of every device in one rack, including a Step Seq pattern. It doesn't change which devices are in the chain. Each of the eight racks has its own 32 pads.

Open a rack's bank ​

From Main Menu → SNAPSHOTS → RACKS, pick a rack.

Or from the Racks list:

  1. Open Main Menu → RACKS.
  2. Go to the rack and hold Hold Select (long press) Select.
  3. Choose SNAPSHOTS BANK.

Take, recall, the pad menu and the pencil tab work as on knob snapshots.

A rack with no devices can't be stored: TAKE shows RACK HAS NO DEVICES. If storage is full, it shows NO FREE SNAPSHOT SLOTS; check Storage Info.

A recalled Step Seq pattern keeps playing from where the playhead is.

With a Delay or Arpeggiator SYNC set to IN SNAPSHOT NO, a recalled DELAY TIME or RATE keeps its length: a stored 1/8 becomes the same time in milliseconds when SYNC is off, and a stored time in milliseconds lands on the nearest note value when SYNC is on.

To keep one device or one parameter as it is on every recall, see Keep something out of snapshots.
