Step Seq

Classic clock-synced step sequencer

When to use it ​

Add Step Seq after a Clock when you want a classic 4–16 step sequencer you edit on a full-screen grid — notes with velocity and gate.

INFO

Needs clock (internal or external). Unlike most devices, Step Seq opens a custom step grid instead of a plain parameter list.

Editing the grid ​

Step Seq opens a full-width step grid (not the usual parameter list). The header shows the Step Seq icon, a dim R#/ and STEP SEQ (rack number, like other devices). Folder tabs SEQ and a pencil sit left of the BPM (never the word SETTINGS). The selected tab is a black tab with a thin outline that opens into the grid; the other one is plain gray text. Two small gray squares with a dotted line under them, between the tabs and the BPM, mean Select + Back switches tabs.

Each step is a rounded pad with its note name. Up to 8 steps fill one row of tall pads; 9–16 steps use two rows. An active step has a brighter frame and a short velocity bar under the note: the louder the step, the wider the bar. A rest has a dim frame and a dim note. The focused step has a White frame and a White note. The playhead is a White bar inside the top of the pad it is playing.

Hold Select and turn the encoder to change the focused step’s note. While you hold, the pad shows corner marks inside its frame and a White line under the note, and the header shows a circle and square. Release Select to keep the note.

STEP panel ​

Long-press Select on a step to open its STEP panel. The panel opens on the half of the screen away from the step, so the step you are editing stays in view: a step on the left half opens the panel on the right, a step on the right half opens it on the left.

Long-press Select on a panel row opens that row’s Options (MODULATION and MAPPING) on the other half, with the STEP panel dimmed beside it. Back closes Options and returns to the panel; Back again closes the panel and stays on SEQ (and restores the field if you were in sticky edit). Select + Back still switches SEQ / pencil.

BYPASS0 – 1When on, the device is disabled and MIDI passes through unchanged.
STEPS4 – 16Number of steps in the pattern (4–16).

Per-step parameters ​

These fields are per step (not in the settings list). Long-press Select on a focused step — the header Select square fills while you hold — to open them in the STEP panel. Each row shows its live value. Tap Select to latch sticky edit, or hold Select and turn. Short Select on the grid still toggles Active. LFO, knob-macro, and external mappings can target a step field with that step’s index.

ParameterRangeWhat it does
Active0 – 1Whether the step fires when the playhead lands on it.
Note0 – 127MIDI note number for this step.
Velocity1 – 127Note-on velocity (also drawn as the short bar under the note name).
Gate10 – 200Note length as a percentage of the step.
Channel0 – 16Per-step channel override (Global uses Seq Ch).
Probability0 – 100Chance the step fires when active (0–100%).

TIP

Steps, Rate, Seq Ch, Direction, and Randomize live on the pencil tab (Select + Back from SEQ).
