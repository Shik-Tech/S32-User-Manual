MIDI Device Reference ​

Devices sit in a rack chain, and MIDI flows through them in order. Select a device on the chain to open its parameters.

Adding a device ​

Main Menu → RACKS → pick a rack → select the + card → pick a device. See Racks.

Editing parameters ​

Parameters edit like any value: see Edit a value. Hold Select on a parameter for its MODULATION and MAPPING (side menus).

A small mark before a value means an LFO, a knob or an external controller moves it: see Modulation and Mapping.

BYPASS ​

BYPASS is the first setting on every device. Turn it ON to take the device out of the chain without deleting it: MIDI passes through as if it weren't there. Use it to hear a part with and without the device, or put it on a knob to drop an arpeggio or a harmony in and out while you play.

  • On: every note the device was playing or had changed gets released, so nothing hangs: an arpeggio, a sequence, Harmony voices, transposed notes, Delay echoes.
  • Off: the device works again from the next note. The Arpeggiator and Step Seq start their pattern from the beginning.
DeviceBYPASS ON
MIDI IN, MIDI OUTMutes the rack: nothing comes in or goes out. Notes still playing stop, and a held sustain pedal is let go.
ClockThe devices after it get no clock, and their notes are released.
LooperRecording and playback stop; the loop is kept. Turn BYPASS off and the loop plays again, unless MODE is STOP.
EnvelopeThe destination goes back to rest: a CC to 0, pitch bend to centre.
M2CVEvery gate closes and the outputs stop following MIDI.

BYPASS is a setting like any other: put it on a knob, move it with an LFO, or store it in a snapshot.

Categories ​

Devices are grouped as on the Add Device picker.

I/O & CLOCK

  • CLOCK — BPM, TRANSPORT, AND CLOCK OUTPUT
  • MIDI IN — RECEIVE MIDI FROM A PORT
  • MIDI OUT — SEND MIDI TO A PORT

GENERATIVE

  • ARPEGGIATOR — ARPEGGIATE HELD NOTES
  • EUCLID — EUCLIDEAN RHYTHM GENERATOR
  • SEED — RANDOM MELODY GENERATOR
  • STEP SEQ — CLASSIC CLOCK-SYNCED STEP SEQUENCER

PITCH & HARMONY

  • HARMONY — ADDS HARMONY NOTES
  • SCALER — QUANTIZE NOTES TO A SCALE
  • TRANSPOSE — SHIFT PITCH BY SEMITONES

FILTERS

  • CC TYPE FILTER — PASS OR BLOCK CC TYPES
  • CC VALUE FILTER — FILTER CC BY VALUE
  • CHANNEL FILTER — FILTER BY MIDI CHANNEL
  • FILTER — PASS OR BLOCK MIDI TYPES
  • NOTE RANGE FILTER — FILTER BY NOTE RANGE

ROUTING

  • DISPATCH — SPREAD NOTES OVER CHANNELS
  • ROUTER — MOVE CHANNELS ONTO ONE

DYNAMICS

  • ENVELOPE — ADSR ENVELOPE ON MIDI
  • LIMITER — KEEP VALUES IN A RANGE
  • VELOCITY AMP — SCALE NOTE VELOCITY

EFFECTS

  • DELAY — DELAY NOTES IN TIME

UTILITY

  • CHANCE — PROBABILISTIC NOTE GATE
  • LOOPER — LOOP AND OVERDUB MIDI
  • M2CV — MIDI TO CONTROL VOLTAGE
  • RANDOM — RANDOMIZE VELOCITY OR A CC
