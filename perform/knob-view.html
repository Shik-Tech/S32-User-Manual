Perform View ​

Perform is the screen you play from. Turn any knob and its four macros send; Perform shows that knob and what each macro is sending.

Controls ​

Do thisWhat happens
Turn a panel knobIts macros send, and Perform shows it
Press Back (short) BackThe Knobs Grid opens
Hold Select (long press) Hold SelectEdit mode opens for the knob on screen
Hold Back and Select together Select + BackThe Main Menu opens

Knobs count down each column: 1–4 in the first column, 5–8 in the next, up to 29–32.

Move several knobs at once and Perform stays on the first one until it stops. The others still send.

Header ​

  • Knob map: the 32 knobs, 1–16 on the left half and 17–32 on the right. The white one is on screen.
  • BPM: the tempo. The two marks next to it flip on every beat while the clock runs. Set it in edit mode.

Macro rows ​

Each row is one macro: what it controls on the left, its value on the right. The marks in between:

MarkMeans
CH1–CH16, GLOBThe MIDI channel. GLOB is the project's channel
U 1 2Lit outputs: USB, MIDI1, MIDI2
~1, L1LFO 1 moves this macro. Dim when that LFO is off
INVThe macro is inverted. Shown when the knob has one or two macros
+An empty macro
SET TARGETThe macro has a mode but no target yet. Finish it in its settings

What each kind of macro shows: Macro rows.

One, two, or three macros ​

A knob with fewer than four macros hides the empty rows and gives the others more room.

Macro indicator bars ​

The bar shows the value between the macro's MINIMUM and MAXIMUM.

A macro with MINIMUM 0 and MAXIMUM 1 is a switch: the knob sends 1 above its middle and 0 below.

Pickup mode ​

A dim value means pickup is waiting. Turn the knob to the bright tick under the bar (a dotted line on a tall bar). It sends nothing until it gets there.

Snapshot and morph knobs ​

A SNAPSHOT knob recalls the snapshots of a bank as you turn it: knobs, LFOs or a rack. A MORPH knob blends toward a snapshot. See Morph.
